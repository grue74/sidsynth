# Listening tests

Recordings in `work/` (not in git), made by `python tools/demo_band.py
work/demo`. SID500 makes no sound of its own: the family's synths play
what it sends.

## Stage C: a song entered by steps (2026-11-22)

`work/demo/demo.mp3`. SID500 in VICE at real speed typed five patterns
into its step grid from a key script (TEST_KEYS=13, generated by
`tools/demo_song.py`), then played them and built them up live. Its MIDI
out was captured; every note on was checked against the patterns and found
on its 16th, with its off. Each synth's part was rendered exactly from the
capture (sidsynth/band's feeds.py and c64tools' vicerec.py, on SID500's
own clock), then mixed with the band's balance (levels.py).
`python tools/demo_band.py --check` checks the typed patterns alone,
headless.

`demo.mp3`: SID500 entered five patterns on its step grid (tools/demo_song.py), then played them at 125 BPM to SID808 (drums, ch 10), SID303 (bass, ch 2) and SID101 (lead, ch 1), built up live. Each synth's part is rendered exactly from what SID500 sent (captured, then sidsynth/band's feeds.py).

| Time | Bar | What | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00.5 | 1 | drums A alone (SID808: kick, clap, closed and open hats) | a steady four on the floor; the hats even, the open hat on the last 8th |
| 0:08.2 | 5 | the bass in | the bass comes in on the bar; its accents (louder, brighter) and slides (gliding into the next note) where the pattern has them |
| 0:15.9 | 9 | the lead in | the lead in time with the drums and the bass, from the bar |
| 0:31.2 | 17 | lead B (switched on the playing scene) | the switch lands where the loop is, no gap, no note doubled or cut short |
| 0:38.9 | 21 | drums B | ghost hats on the 16ths, the snare roll at the end of each second bar |
| 0:46.6 | 25 | the drums out (a break) | the drums stop cleanly (a mute is heard within ~42 ms); bass and lead keep time alone |
| 0:50.4 | 27 | the drums back | the drums back on the beat |
| 0:54.3 | 29 | lead A again | the first lead again, as at 0:15 |
| 1:01.9 | 33 | stop | everything stops; no note left hanging |
